How to use: Filter by Dataset (EcopanGEM / LactoPanGEM) or Organism, or search the table. Click a GEM File to inspect its reactions, metabolites and genes in the browser. Select rows and use Download Selected GEMs to fetch just those models (packaged client-side, no server), or Download All GEMs for the full archives. Every model is a COBRApy-compatible JSON.

E. coli: EcopanGEM · Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.17581962 · Ardalani et al., "Annotating the Pangenome Reveals the Diversity in the Genetic Basis for Metabolic Enzymes"
Lactobacillaceae: LactoPanGEM · Ardalani et al., "Pangenome-Scale Reconstruction of Lactobacillaceae Metabolism", mSystems (2024) · metadata from NCBI Datasets
